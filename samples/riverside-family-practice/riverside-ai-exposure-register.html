Executive Summary

A survey of Riverside's 12 physicians and 5 administrative staff, cross-referenced against OAuth-connected third-party applications in the practice's Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 admin consoles, identified 14 distinct AI tools in active use. Three are approved, tenant-governed tools with executed BAAs and DPAs. Two are in use under a restriction (no PHI permitted). The remaining nine are either unapproved consumer-tier tools in active use by clinical or administrative staff, or approved vendors being used at the wrong tier without the required agreements in place.

Two tools — Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai — are currently being used to record and transcribe live patient conversations and clinical case discussions without a Business Associate Agreement. Zoom AI Companion is auto-generating telehealth visit summaries under the same condition. These three represent the highest-priority items in the remediation plan below.

3. Acceptable Use Policy — Template

Drafted for adoption by Riverside leadership; placeholders in highlight require practice-specific input before distribution. Final policy should be reviewed by the practice's legal counsel.

Artificial Intelligence Acceptable Use Policy

Practice: [PRACTICE LEGAL NAME]   Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]   Policy owner: [NAME / TITLE, e.g., Practice Manager]   Review cycle: [ANNUALLY / SEMI-ANNUALLY], next review [REVIEW DATE]

1. Purpose

This policy governs the use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including generative AI chatbots, transcription services, and AI-enabled features within approved software, by all employees, contractors, and affiliated providers of [PRACTICE NAME]. Its purpose is to prevent unauthorized disclosure of protected health information (PHI) and to establish a clear, auditable process for evaluating and approving new AI tools.

2. Approved Tools

Only tools listed on the current Approved AI Tool List may be used for any task involving patient information. As of [EFFECTIVE DATE], the Approved AI Tool List consists of:

  • [Microsoft 365 Copilot — tenant license, scheduling and internal correspondence]
  • [Google Gemini (Workspace) — tenant license, patient-facing email drafting]
  • [Dragon Medical One — clinical dictation into the EHR]

Tools marked "Restricted" may be used only for the specific non-PHI purpose documented in the current AI Data Exposure Register (e.g., [Canva for patient-education design work, with no patient identifiers entered]). No other AI tool may be used for any work purpose unless formally approved under Section 4.

3. Prohibited Conduct

  • Entering any patient name, date of birth, medical record number, diagnosis, treatment detail, photograph, or other PHI into any AI tool not on the Approved AI Tool List.
  • Using a personal (non-practice-issued) account to access any AI tool for a work purpose.
  • Recording or transcribing any patient conversation, visit, or clinical discussion using a tool without an executed BAA.
  • Installing or enabling browser extensions, add-ins, or AI features in approved software without prior written approval.

4. Requesting a New Tool

Employees who wish to use an AI tool not on the Approved AI Tool List must submit a request to [POLICY OWNER] describing the intended use, the data involved, and the vendor. No tool may be used for PHI-related work until it has a reviewed BAA (where PHI is involved), a signed DPA, and has been added to the Approved AI Tool List in writing.

5. Training & Acknowledgment

All employees and providers must complete AI acceptable use training within 30 days of hire and upon any material policy update, and must sign the acknowledgment below.

6. Enforcement

Violations of this policy will be addressed under [PRACTICE NAME]'s standard HR disciplinary process and, where applicable, reported under the practice's HIPAA breach notification procedure.

Four two-to-four-week phases from October 28, 2026 to January 26, 2027, ordered by risk. Each item names a specific action (block, restrict, upgrade, replace, or decommission) and an owner-ready deadline.

Oct 28 – November 11, 2026 · due November 11, 2026
