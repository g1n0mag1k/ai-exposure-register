# AI Data Exposure Register — Pricing

Sole-proprietor service under Sui-Generis (Andrew Rogers), Knoxville, TN.

## Core engagement

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct engagement** | **$2,000** | AI tool inventory (staff survey + Google Workspace/M365 OAuth enumeration), BAA/DPA matrix, acceptable-use policy template, 90-day remediation plan. |
| **White-label (via law firm)** | **$1,200–1,500** | Same deliverable, subcontracted through an attorney who retains the client relationship and attaches the work product to their own advice. No client-list requests, no referral fees (ABA Rule 1.6 / 5.4(a)). |

## Recurring

| Tier | Price | What it is |

|---|---|---|

## Why these numbers

Positioned against Black Chili (£3,000–£7,000 workshop model), The Data
Experts ($2,500+), and MSP Copilot Readiness assessments ($4,800–$50,000,
Microsoft-only) — priced below those, on the strength of the healthtech/law-
firm vertical specialization and faster delivery via the OAuth enumeration +
cross-reference automation already built in `scripts/` and `ai-tool-
compliance-db`.

## Guardrails that apply to every tier

- Never say "compliant" or "in violation" — that's a legal conclusion.
- Client data never enters a public repo or consumer AI tier.
- Client signs their own deletion letters (template prepared by Sui-Generis).
- One-time privacy attorney review of disclaimers before the first law firm
  client.
